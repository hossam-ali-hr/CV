How this calculator works

  • Service uses full anniversaries plus the fraction of the current service year. Date intervals run from the start up to, but excluding, the selected end date. Leap years are included.
  • EOS uses final basic salary ÷ 30 × EOS days per year × service years, after one continuous year. Leave accrual splits at service anniversaries, including the five-year threshold.
  • Unused leave = opening balance + accrual − used days + approved adjustment. Negative balances are shown for review and are not automatically deducted from gratuity.
  • Monthly amounts use a 30-day divisor. Before-leave payment and termination estimates are separate scenarios. Reconcile any housing advance against payroll; this page has no payment history.

Indicative estimate for ordinary monthly-paid private-sector employment under Qatar Labour Law. Special regimes, piecework, dismissal exceptions, prior gratuity payments, notice pay, loans, final salary and travel tickets are not assessed. Confirm the applicable contract, records and rules before payment.

Reference: Qatar Labour Law No. 14 of 2004 — Articles 54, 72, 79 and 81. Calculator version: 21 October 2026.
