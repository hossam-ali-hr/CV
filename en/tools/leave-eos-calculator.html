Wifaq · Manual calculator

Qatar leave & end-of-service calculator

Enter service start, last return and salary. Customize allowances and holidays, then calculate the itemized estimate.

Inputs are processed in this page only. No upload, account, browser storage or server submission. No employee name or ID is needed.

1. Service & salary
Used for EOS and the five-year leave rate; never reset on return.
2. Leave cycle: last return
Required by default. No fallback to hire date if left blank.

When the leave-payment preview is enabled, accrual ends on departure. Otherwise it ends on the calculation date. A new return date does not erase carried leave: enter any confirmed carry-in balance.

3. Annual policy & encashment
For example 3 extra days under an approved policy, prorated over this cycle.

The five-year rate uses total continuous service, even when this leave cycle starts from last return. Extra company days are a policy benefit, not an automatic statutory addition.

4. Outstanding public-holiday settlement

Enter previously reviewed, unpaid/uncredited holiday items only. Cash and leave credit are alternatives. This is not an automatic holiday-work or overtime entitlement calculation; the factor/amount must already be approved.

5. Allowance payment policy

These payment choices are independent of the unused-leave encashment basis above. Fixed amounts must be net of any sums paid elsewhere.

6. Upcoming leave & housing advance
Use YYYY-MM-DD. Enter confirmed dates inside departure/return only; no dates are guessed automatically. On these dates the calculator shows full cash wage separately, without paying the same allowances again.

The work-return day is excluded. Housing already covered by the advance is removed from annual-leave and holiday payments for the same dates. Days above the paid balance are shown separately.

How this calculator works

  • EOS uses continuous service from hire to the calculation date. Annual leave starts from last return by default; first-leave and verified-balance modes are explicit alternatives. When an upcoming leave is entered, accrual stops at departure. The five-year leave-rate threshold always follows continuous service.
  • Accrual can use actual service-year length, actual / 365 or 30 / 360. Date intervals exclude the end date. EOS keeps the full-year/prorated-service method and basic salary ÷ 30.
  • Annual balance, approved compensatory leave and outstanding holiday cash are itemized. Compensatory balance is not automatically cashed out on termination. Transport, housing and other allowances each have an independent leave-payment rule.
  • Holiday dates inside leave use full cash wage once. Excluding dates from the leave debit or crediting replacement days are bookkeeping choices requiring review of applicable rules. Previously worked holidays use only manually approved settlement inputs. Company enhancements do not remove statutory or contractual rights.
  • Leave and EOS payments are alternative scenarios. Reconcile past housing payments manually: this page keeps no payment records or employee data.

Indicative estimate for ordinary monthly-paid private-sector employment under Qatar Labour Law. Special regimes, piecework, dismissal exceptions, prior gratuity payments, notice pay, loans, final salary and travel tickets are not assessed. Confirm the applicable contract, records and rules before payment.

Reference: Qatar Labour Law No. 14 of 2004 — Articles 54, 72, 78, 79 and 81. Calculator version: 21 October 2026.
