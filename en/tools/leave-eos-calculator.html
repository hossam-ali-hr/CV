How the estimate worksDate boundaries, payment assumptions and source.

Leave runs from departure through the day before return. The accrual cycle begins at last return by default; original hire date still controls the five-year band and EOS. Negative balances are shown for review, with no automatic deduction from gratuity.

Current-month salary covers eligible calendar payroll days before departure, or through the last working day on exit. It includes paid rest days and all entered monthly cash allowances. Unpaid absence reduces it proportionally. The selected daily divisor applies. Previously counted salary dates, earlier planned leave and covered housing advances are excluded to avoid duplicate payments.

Two periods carry one balance forward. Prorated cash tickets use paid annual leave days, capped at the unused allocation. Company-provided tickets remain outside employee cash. Housing advances and confirmed holidays are counted once. Leave allowance rules apply independently from normal working-month salary.

An estimate for ordinary monthly paid private-sector employees under Qatar Labour Law. Verify the contract, approvals and payment records before disbursement. Excludes notice pay, loans, prior gratuity payments and special employment regimes. Below one year, gratuity is zero and projected leave eligibility needs review.

Source: Qatar Labour Law No. 14 of 2004 — Articles 54, 68, 72, 78–81. Reviewed 21 October 2026.
